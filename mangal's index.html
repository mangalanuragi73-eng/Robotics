Robotics is the interdisciplinary field of engineering and computer science dedicated to the design, construction, operation, and use of robots. These machines are programmed to replicate or substitute human actions, ranging from simple repetitive tasks to complex decision-making in hazardous or precise environments. A robot typically consists of four core components: a mechanical structure for physical form, actuators (muscles) for movement, sensors for environmental perception, and a control system (brain) that processes data to direct actions.
